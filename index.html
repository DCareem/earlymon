よくある質問

Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. It's which cards Japan is winning with and what they cost. Prices go down too.

Why Japan?

Japan gets each set 3 to 4 months early and publishes its City League decklists. What wins there usually gets copied in English once the cards come out.

When does the paid list start?

Once a rise in the new season holds for more than one weekend. People on the waitlist hear first, and you only pay if you decide to subscribe then.

Where do the prices come from?

TCGplayer (US) and Cardmarket (Europe) market prices, through Limitless TCG.
