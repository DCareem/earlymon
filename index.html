Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. It is information about which cards are being played in Japan and their public prices. Card prices can fall as well as rise.

Why Japan?

Japan releases each set 3 to 4 months earlier and publishes its City League decklists. Decks that win there tend to be copied in English once the cards arrive.

When does the paid list start?

Now that Japan's new season has started, the first confirmed picks follow once a rise holds for more than one weekend. Waitlist members hear first. You pay nothing until you choose to subscribe at launch.

Where do the prices come from?

Public market prices from TCGplayer (US) and Cardmarket (Europe), via Limitless TCG.
